# 📊 Stock Market Analysis Dashboard (GOOGL)

A complete **end-to-end stock market analytics dashboard** built to transform raw financial data into **actionable insights** using data analytics and visualization techniques.

This project focuses not just on charts, but on **decision-making clarity**, simulating how analytics is applied in real-world business and investment scenarios.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview

The dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of **Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL)** stock performance using historical market data and technical indicators.

It answers key questions such as:
- How is the stock trending over time?
- What is the current market momentum?
- How volatile is the stock?
- How does it perform compared to major indices?
- What insights can help support buy/sell decisions?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Power BI**
  - Data modeling
  - DAX calculations
  - Interactive dashboards
  - KPI cards & custom visuals

- **Python**
  - Pandas – data cleaning & transformation  
  - NumPy – numerical calculations  

- **Data Source**
  - Yahoo Finance (historical stock market data)

- **Technical Indicators**
  - Simple Moving Averages (50-day & 200-day)
  - RSI (Relative Strength Index)
  - MACD
  - Bollinger Bands
  - Volume & Returns Analysis

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Price Trends & Moving Averages
- Line chart showing closing price
- 50-day and 200-day SMA to identify trend direction

### 2. Trading Volume Analysis
- Daily trading volume visualization
- Volume spikes to identify unusual activity

### 3. Volatility Analysis (Bollinger Bands)
- Upper, middle, and lower bands
- Helps identify overbought and oversold conditions

### 4. Momentum Indicators
- RSI to track momentum strength
- MACD to detect trend reversals

### 5. Performance Metrics
- 52-week high & low
- Average trading volume
- Beta and P/E ratio

### 6. Market Comparison
- Performance comparison with:
  - S&P 500
  - NASDAQ
  - Technology Sector
- Correlation and alpha metrics

### 7. AI-Based Insights (Rule-Based Logic)
- Trend signal (Bullish/Bearish)
- Support and resistance levels
- 12-month price target
- Investment recommendation
